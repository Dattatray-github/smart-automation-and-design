About Us - Smart Automation and Design

Smart Automation And Design Your Partner in Industrial Automation, Electrical Design & Technical Training.

Smart Automation And Design is focused on delivering practical, reliable, and innovative solutions in industrial automation, electrical engineering, and technical education. Our goal is to help industries improve productivity and help aspiring engineers develop industry-ready automation skills.

⚙️

Expertise

We specialize in PLC programming, HMI development, SCADA systems, EPLAN electrical design, and automation training. By combining engineering knowledge with practical implementation, we aim to bridge the gap between theoretical learning and real-world industrial applications.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide quality automation solutions and practical technical training that empower industries, engineers, and students to achieve greater efficiency, productivity, and professional growth.

🛡️

Our Vision

To become a trusted name in industrial automation, electrical design, and technical training by delivering innovative solutions and developing the next generation of skilled automation professionals.

🎓

Our Commitment

At Smart Automation And Design, we believe in making automation smarter and technical learning more practical. Whether you are an industry looking for automation expertise or an engineer looking to upgrade your skills, we aim to support your journey toward success.

    Our Services

  • PLC Programming: Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi, and other industrial control platforms.
  • HMI Development: User-friendly operator interfaces for industrial machines and processes.
  • SCADA Systems: Industrial monitoring, process visualization, alarms, and data acquisition.
